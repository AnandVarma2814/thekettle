The Kettle

The Kettle makes short, original explainer videos about topics trending in the US (technology, science, money, sports and entertainment) and publishes them to its own YouTube channel. Every video is drawn by code and narrated with a synthetic voice; its facts are checked against the news sources listed in its description.

The Kettle app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API only for its own channel: to upload the videos it creates and to read how they perform, so it can make better ones. It does not access anyone else's account.
