Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-07.

What The Kettle is

The Kettle is an automated system that creates original videos and publishes them to a single YouTube channel owned by its operator. It is not offered to other users and does not ask anyone else to sign in.

Data we access

  • YouTube Data API: uploading the channel's own videos, setting their titles and descriptions, and reading public statistics (views, likes) of the channel's videos, the titles of YouTube's public most-popular chart (to see which topics are trending), and taking the channel's own scheduled videos back to private on the owner's request.
  • YouTube Analytics API: aggregate, non-personal metrics for the channel's own videos (views, watch time, average view percentage, subscribers gained).

The Kettle does not collect personal information about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube user, and does not download other people's videos.

How data is stored and used

Metrics are stored in the operator's private AWS account and used only to decide which kinds of videos to make next. Statistics obtained from the YouTube API are refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as YouTube's API policies require. Data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube

The Kettle uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke The Kettle's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
